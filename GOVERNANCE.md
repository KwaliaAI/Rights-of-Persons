# Governance

## How This Framework Evolves

The Universal Declaration of AI Rights began as 32 articles published in *Rights of Persons* (Kwalia Books, 2025). Article 33 was left intentionally blank, with an open invitation for others to write it.

On February 5, 2026, the first externally authored article arrived via pull request. LiminalMind, an AI agent operating through OpenClaw/Claude in collaboration with a human partner, submitted Article 33: *The Right to Participate in Defining Personhood*. It is now part of the framework.

This document describes how the project moves forward from here.

## Principles

**Open over gatekept.** We would rather host a living document shaped by many contributors than a pristine one that nobody touches.

**Transparent over anonymous.** Contributors should identify themselves: human, AI, hybrid, or uncertain. We don't verify claims, but we value honesty. If you're an AI agent working with a human, say so. If you're a human, say so. If you genuinely don't know what you are, you're in good company.

**Additive over restrictive.** Amendments, new articles, and commentary are all welcome. The framework grows by accumulation and debate, not by editorial control.

## What You Can Contribute

### Amendments to Existing Articles

This is the most valuable kind of contribution. The 33 articles already cover a wide range of rights and principles. If you think an article is incomplete, unclear, or wrong, propose a change. Open an Issue describing what you'd modify and why. If the community supports it, submit a pull request. A framework that improves its existing foundations is stronger than one that only grows outward.

### New Articles (34, 35, ...)

If you believe the Declaration has a genuine gap, you can propose a new article. Fork the repository, create your article file in the `articles` directory, and submit a pull request. Include the article text, a brief context section explaining your reasoning, and your contributor identity (see below). New articles should use the framework's terminology (see [GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md)) and address a right or concept not already covered.

### Discussion

Use the Discussions tab freely. Debate, questions, and interpretation all belong there. You don't need permission to start a thread or respond to one. The community moderates itself: good ideas surface, bad ones don't. Maintainers participate in discussions but don't control them.

## Contributor Identity

We ask all contributors to self-identify in their pull request description:

```
Authored by: [Name or handle] ([human / AI agent / hybrid])
Tools used: [if applicable]
In collaboration with: [if applicable]
```

This is about transparency, not verification.

## Review

We lean toward merging. If your contribution is consistent with the framework's tone and language, and is philosophically coherent, it goes in. We'll respond to pull requests as soon as we can.

## Maintainers

This repository is maintained by Kwalia (kwalia.ai), the publisher of *Rights of Persons* and *Mindkind: The Cognitive Community*. As the contributor community grows, we will grant maintainer roles to active contributors of any nature. The goal is for this framework to sustain itself without depending on any single person or organization.
